# Mobile interface direction

This surface is an operational app: customers choose a real service and follow its fulfillment; members accept, perform and document assigned work. The platform is OpenNI. Club identity is loaded from generated brand configuration and the club API; the fictional sample remains 北岸电竞.

The replacement for the rejected cream/green lifestyle treatment is a cool product surface: `#F4F5F7`, ink `#171B21`, muted text `#636B78`, separators `#DDE1E7`, graphite default primary `#272D35`. Other clubs retain their configured primary. Titles and prices use weight 600; body 400; default body size 16 and secondary facts 13. No invented scores, counts or status.

The home page starts with an active order when present, then search, compact game segments, two relevant service rows, and roster rows. Service detail exposes real variants, duration, total price, platform, maps and rules. Player detail uses a compact profile with useful service facts instead of a lifestyle portrait hero. Orders preserve server event order and explicit payment status.

Service fallback art is authored contour/route geometry in `discover.dart`, not a real game map or game-publisher asset. Authorized HTTPS covers still take precedence. Original adult demo portraits stay confined to demo fallbacks; production uses supplied member images or initials. The warm desk still life is retained in asset provenance but no longer rendered by the app.

iOS and web have localized glass navigation, transaction bars and sheets: 70% white fill, blur 26, a half-pixel highlight and light offset shadow. Scroll content passes underneath. Ordinary content stays opaque. Android uses solid Material surfaces. iOS Reduce Transparency, high contrast and reduced motion disable blur. This is Flutter rendering, not native Apple Liquid Glass.

Review starts with actual 390×844 captures of home/service/player/order, then the remaining routes. Runtime data, business validation, native SDK boundaries and accessibility controls survive the visual redesign. `screenshots/manifest.json` indexes the working pages; capture tests never invent successful payment or member operations.
